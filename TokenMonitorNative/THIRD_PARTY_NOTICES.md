# Third-party references

Token Monitor: https://github.com/Javis603/token-monitor (MIT license).
This independent client interoperates with its v0.56.0 Hub API. The matched-counter rate algorithm follows the behavior of `src/electron/renderer/tokenRatePresentation.js`. The Electron application, its branding assets, account integrations and collection code are not bundled here. Synthetic fixtures are generated locally from the documented field shapes.

SwiftUI, AppKit, Foundation, Security and Swift Charts are Apple system frameworks. Sparkle is referenced as a future integration and is not bundled or enabled.
